<h3>Privacy Policy</h3>

<h4>Last updated: April 1, 2025</h4>

<p>We collect your email address, name, and basic profile data required to register and use the app. Additionally, we store information related to your calorie intake and personal health metrics you input into the system.</p>

<b>How We Use Your Data</b>

<p>Your data is used exclusively to provide and personalize your experience within the app, such as calculating your daily caloric needs or saving your meal history. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.</p>

<b>Data Security</b>

<p>All personal data is stored securely in encrypted databases. We use industry-standard practices to ensure that your information remains private and protected.</p>

<b>Your Rights</b>

<p>You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting our support team or using the account settings.</p>

<b>Contact</b>
